01

Redesign Goals

What this UI revision must achieve, stated as testable requirements.
G1

Reduce time per record

The entry flow must not add steps beyond photo → review → save. Layout changes are evaluated against the speed metric in the study protocol.

Measure: mean open-to-submit time, experimental group.
G2

Make value origin visible

Every field must show whether a human confirmed it or the model proposed it, without relying on color alone.

Measure: reviewers identify unconfirmed fields at a glance in usability checks.
G3

Support long sessions

Staff work through hundreds of records per sitting. Density must stay readable and controls must stay reachable at all viewport sizes.

Measure: 40px minimum targets, AA contrast, stable table layout at 1000 rows.
G4

One system, no per-page styling

Dashboard, entry, admin, settings, and auth consume the same tokens and components. Page-level overrides are not permitted.

Measure: all color, type, and spacing referenced through tokens.
02

Design Principles

The rules to apply when the spec doesn't cover a case.
P1

Structure first, color last

Hierarchy comes from size, weight, spacing, and 1px rules. If a screen needs color to look organized, fix the layout instead.

P2

Two typefaces, no more

Inter for everything read as language. JetBrains Mono for everything read as data: IDs, phones, dates, prices, timestamps, tokens.

P3

Color encodes origin

Green = confirmed by a person. Purple = produced by the model, unconfirmed. All other UI is black, white, and gray. Accents never exceed 5% of a screen.

P4

Nothing decorative

No gradients, no shadows at rest, no illustrations in the work surface, no motion outside hover and focus. Every pixel is information or affordance.

07

Usage Rules

Non-negotiable. Design review checks against this list.

Do

  • Pair every use of green or purple with a text label; color never carries meaning alone.
  • Set tabular data in mono so columns align: IDs, phones, dates, prices, timestamps.
  • Reserve green for the single commit action on a view (Confirm & verify / Save).
  • Use purple strictly for model-originated content and the actions that accept it.
  • Keep 4px radius, 1px borders, and the 4px spacing scale; hit targets at least 40px.
  • Meet WCAG 2.1 AA: all text pairings specified here clear 4.5:1 in both themes.

Don’t

  • Don’t fill headers, navigation, or sidebars with green or purple.
  • Don’t use purple for human-entered content or green for mere selection states.
  • Don’t add drop shadows, gradients, illustrations, or scroll-triggered animation.
  • Don’t introduce a third typeface or a page-level style override.
  • Don’t use official UT Dallas marks, logos, or the university wordmark without sponsorship; this palette is an homage, not a license.
  • Don’t rely on hover-only affordances; every action must be reachable by keyboard.
  • Don’t use em dashes in any interface copy, labels, hints, or documentation. Use a colon, a comma, or a full stop instead.
